Overview

A rhythm game where you play the king's jester, performing ridiculous dance moves on a wooden board to entertain the court and survive — fail to amuse, and it's off with your head. Built in 48 hours at the Global Game Jam 2024, on the theme "Make Me Laugh." Developed the core gameplay mechanics.

Highlights

  • Global Game Jam 2024 — "Make Me Laugh"
  • Rhythm/timing-based dance mechanic
  • Built and shipped in 48 hours
